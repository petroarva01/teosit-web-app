My RV is our app for anyone travelling by motorhome, campervan or caravan. We built it around the three questions you ask every day on the road: **where to sleep tonight, where to fill up with water, fuel and LPG, and where to empty the tanks** — and how much of each you have left.

The app is free, has no ads and is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teosit.myrv) across Europe, the USA and Canada — 40 countries in total. The iOS version is coming soon. Here's what's inside.

## The map: campsites, parking and service points in one place

The map holds about 450,000 places from OpenStreetMap, refreshed every week, plus spots, reviews and photos added by campers themselves:

- campsites, motorhome parking, overnight spots and wild spots,
- fresh water points and dump stations for grey and black water,
- fuel stations and LPG refills.

For every place you see what's there — electricity, water, dump, showers, toilets, Wi-Fi, dogs, LPG, AdBlue, level ground — the price and opening info when they're known, and reviews with photos from other campers. A "Still correct?" check helps keep the information fresh.

Search works by place or town name, and understands plain words too: in "dump station Lyon" or "free parking dogs", words like "water", "free" and "dogs" become filters. The "Near you" list shows what's around you, and spots you save with the heart stay available offline. Once you pick a place, one tap opens it in Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

## Tank levels without sensors

Tank gauges in motorhomes are famously unreliable — or missing altogether. So My RV **estimates** your levels on the phone, with no sensors at all:

- you add your vehicle once, from a model catalog or by hand: dimensions, fresh water, grey water, fuel and LPG bottles,
- you say how many people travel and how you use water — showers, cooking,
- from that, your fill-ups and the kilometres you drive, the app works out what's left.

Right in the setup you see roughly how long a full fresh water tank lasts you. Estimates are marked with "~" until you confirm them, and when you fill or empty at a service point, one tap — "Filled up" or "Emptied" — brings back the real level.

While the app is open, it points you to fuel, LPG, water or a dump station nearby when you're about to need one.

## Trip planning

You build a trip from several stops: add them from search, from the map or from saved spots, set the nights and arrival date for each, and drag to reorder. The app estimates distance and fuel from your own consumption and suggests stops along the way.

You can send the whole trip to your favourite navigation app. And before you drive off there's a checklist — step in, roof vent closed, gas off — that you can make your own.

## Costs and reminders

- **Costs:** fuel, campsites, tolls, LPG, service — per trip and per month, in multiple currencies.
- **Reminders:** registration, insurance, service, gas inspection and tyres, by date or by kilometres, with notifications.

Everything works in metric or imperial units, and you don't need an account: most of the app works as a guest. You only need one to contribute — to write a review with up to 5 photos, add a new spot or correct an existing one.

## My RV Pro

The essentials are free. For people who travel a lot there's **My RV Pro**, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan:

- **Offline maps** — download a whole country or region (US states, German Länder…), and the map, search and spot details keep working with no signal.
- **Background alerts** — even with the app closed, it tells you when fuel is running low and where the next station is, and when you stop at a water point it asks whether to update your tanks. No continuous GPS, so it's easy on the battery.
- **"Fits my camper" filter** — hides places that are too short, too low or can't take your vehicle's weight, plus advanced filters (e.g. must have electricity + water + dump).
- **Multiple vehicles** and **full cost statistics**: a year view, categories, real fuel consumption in L/100 km or mpg, and CSV export.

## Who My RV is for

For motorhome and caravan owners, for people renting a camper for the holidays, and for anyone living the van life — wherever "where tonight, and is there water?" is part of every day. Overnight parking rules differ from country to country, so always check the local rules.
