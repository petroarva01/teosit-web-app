Hortium is our app for anyone with a vegetable garden, raised beds in the backyard or a few pots on the balcony. It answers the questions every gardener asks week after week: **what can I sow now, when will it be ready, what needs watering and is there frost tonight** — for the place where your garden actually is.

The app is free, with no ads, no account and no tracking. It's on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teosit.hortium) in seven languages: English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian and Serbian. Here's what's inside.

## It starts with your location

First you pick your town. From that, Hortium estimates your **last spring frost and first autumn frost**, and works out everything else from them — because "sow in March" doesn't mean the same thing in Lisbon, Munich and the mountains. It works in the northern and southern hemisphere, in metric or imperial units.

Place search runs on the phone itself, and your garden's location never leaves the device. The weather service only gets an approximate position, rounded to about 1 km.

Then you tick what's already growing. Hortium estimates the dates, and you can adjust them later.

## Today: weather and garden jobs

The first screen is "Your garden today":

- **the weather** for your place, with a note when rain is coming — "Rain on Sunday, about 8 mm. No need to water that day.",
- **today's tasks** — watering, feeding and harvesting, based on what you've planted, which you tick off as you go,
- **what to sow and plant this month**,
- and **the next harvest**, with the days left until it.

## Sowing and harvest calendar

The calendar shows, month by month, when to sow indoors, sow outdoors, plant out, plant and harvest — tuned to your frost dates. Switch to "Harvest" to see what's coming, or search for a specific plant.

## 150 plants, with everything you need to know

For every plant — vegetables, herbs, berries, fruit trees and flowers — Hortium tells you:

- whether it's time to sow or harvest now, and how long that window lasts,
- the time to harvest, sowing depth, spacing and row spacing,
- how much sun it needs, how hard it is to grow, how to water and feed it,
- when it's ready to pick,
- and its **good and bad neighbours** — what grows well with it and what to keep away. These are traditional pairings, so the app shows them as suggestions.

The advice is checked against public growing guides, and every source is listed in the app.

## My garden

When you sow or plant something, you add it to your garden: the plant, the variety (say, San Marzano), whether you sowed or planted, when, in which bed and how many. Hortium shows the expected harvest straight away and sets up watering and feeding reminders.

Each planting moves through stages — seedling, growing, almost ready, harvesting — with a progress bar. You log harvests in kilograms or pounds, so at the end of the season you know what your garden gave you. Anything you plan for later waits under "Planned".

## Bed planner

The planner is a top-down view of your beds, split into squares. Create a bed at its real size, pick a plant and tap the squares where it goes. If you put two plants that don't get along next to each other, Hortium warns you.

Every year has its own layout, and you can start next season from this year's layout — which makes crop rotation much easier.

## Reminders, alerts and widgets

- **One daily summary** at the time you choose — what to water, feed, sow or pick.
- **Frost and heat alerts** — "Frost possible tonight. Cover tender plants before bed."
- **Watering is skipped when it rains.**
- **Widgets** on the home and lock screen: today's tasks, the next harvest and what to sow this month, without opening the app.

All notifications are scheduled by the phone itself, with no server.

## Your garden stays yours

Hortium has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything — your garden, beds, harvests and settings — is stored only on your phone, and the internet is needed only for the weather forecast. The rest works in the middle of the garden with no signal.

So you don't lose your garden with your phone, one button saves a backup file to Google Drive, iCloud or Files, and another restores it.

If you like the app, you can also support it from inside, once or monthly. It's entirely optional — every feature stays free, and support helps the plant library grow.
