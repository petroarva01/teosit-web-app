## What we collect and why

### When you contact us

If you send the contact form or email us, we receive what you choose to share: your name, email address, company, budget range, the services you're interested in, your message and the language version of the site you used.

We use this only to reply to you and to discuss a possible project. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering inquiries. Please don't send confidential details through the form; we're happy to sign an NDA first.

### Analytics, with your consent

If you allow analytics cookies, we use **Google Analytics 4** to see which pages are visited, how visitors find the site and which device and browser they use. Google Analytics doesn't store full IP addresses. We don't use it for advertising and we don't combine it with data from the contact form.

The legal basis is your consent. Without it, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

### Marketing, with your consent

If you allow marketing cookies, we use the **LinkedIn Insight Tag** to measure the results of our LinkedIn ads and to show ads to people who visited the site. LinkedIn may link the visit to your LinkedIn account on its side; we only see aggregated reports, such as job titles or industries of visitors, never individual profiles.

The legal basis is your consent. Without it, the LinkedIn tag is not loaded at all.

### Technical data

When you open any website, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Our hosting provider processes this data to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and keeps it only for a short time. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website.

## Your rights

Under the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy,
- have inaccurate data corrected,
- have your data deleted,
- restrict or object to processing,
- receive your data in a portable format,
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing done before.

To use any of these rights, email [office@teosit.com](mailto:office@teosit.com). You can also file a complaint with the [Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection](https://www.poverenik.rs) in Serbia, or with the data protection authority in your EU country.
